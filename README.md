# Rowmark

Rowmark is a row counter for knitters and crocheters whose hands are busy. A project holds linked
counters: past its length a Row returns to 1 and carries into its Repeat, which can carry into a
Section; a Stitch counter can reset each row; Total rows never rolls over. The counter screen hears
taps on its lower half, volume up, and a Bluetooth camera shutter remote (volume up, Enter or numpad
Enter), and the pattern screen hears the same keys plus its own count button, so the ruler climbs
while the chart is on screen; every count is a haptic tick and an undoable event, and a bell and a buzz ring when an "every
N rows" alert comes round. The free tier has unlimited projects, a home screen widget and a backup
file. One optional one-time unlock adds the pattern viewer (PDF or photo, with a row ruler that moves
as you count and a highlighter), shaping schedules, time and stats per session, a Quick Settings tile,
CSV export of the journal, and a yarn and needle stash.

Everything runs on the device. The app declares no network permission and sends nothing anywhere.

The app is free. One optional one-time purchase (`full_unlock`) unlocks everything, through Google
Play Billing: the Play Billing Library talks to the Google Play Store app on the phone, and the
INTERNET permission its telemetry dependency would add is removed in `AndroidManifest.xml`. The
unlock is read back from Google Play on every start and cached in DataStore (`entitlement`), which is
left out of backups.

## Build

Requires JDK 17 and the Android SDK with platform 36.

```bash
./gradlew testDebugUnitTest
./gradlew assembleDebug
./gradlew bundleRelease       # needs keystore.properties, see below
```

`keystore.properties` and the `.jks` are not committed. Without them the release build stays
unsigned instead of failing:

```properties
storeFile=<slug>-upload.jks
storePassword=...
keyAlias=<slug>
keyPassword=...
```

## Layout

```
app/src/main/kotlin/com/mohdshayan/rowmark/
  App.kt, MainActivity.kt   process and activity entry points
  di/                       ServiceLocator, the manual dependency container
  data/prefs/               DataStore settings (AppPrefs)
  data/db/                  Room database, entities and DAOs (when used)
  core/                     pure Kotlin, no Android imports: CounterEngine, KeyDebouncer,
                            ScheduleEngine, RulerMath, SessionClock, StatsCalc, BackupCodec,
                            CsvWriter, Gates (all JVM-tested)
  data/repo/                ProjectRepository (counts, undo, sessions), PatternRepository
                            (files under filesDir/patterns by hash, PdfRenderer), StashRepository,
                            BackupRepository (zip of backup.json plus patterns/ and photos/)
  billing/                  Entitlement rules, UnlockRepository (the one BillingClient)
  input/                    KeyRouter: volume and remote keys reach the open counter
  feedback/                 the tick, the bell (res/raw/repeat_bell.ogg) and the buzz
  widget/, tile/            the home screen widget and the Quick Settings tile
  review/                   the once-only in-app review prompt
  ui/theme/                 colour, type, shape and motion tokens, AppTheme
  ui/nav/                   AppNav and the type-safe routes
  ui/projects, ui/counter, ui/projectedit, ui/pattern, ui/stats, ui/stash, ui/settings, ui/unlock
  ui/components/            Drum, Teaser, EmptyState, CountKeys (volume and remote keys, keep awake),
                            Locked (LocalUnlock, lockedAction, UnlockGate)
app/src/debug/, app/src/release/   DebugUnlock: a screenshot-only tier override in debug, null in release
app/src/test/, app/src/testRelease/  JVM unit tests
tools/                      make_bell.py and make_sample_chart.py, which produced the bundled assets
store/                      Play listing copy, icon, feature graphic, screenshots
docs/                       privacy policy and font licences
```

## Permissions

`android.permission.VIBRATE` for the tick and the alert buzz, and `com.android.vending.BILLING`
(merged by the Play Billing Library) for the optional unlock. INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE are
removed from the merged manifest. The Quick Settings tile's `BIND_QUICK_SETTINGS_TILE` is a guard on
the service, not a permission the app requests.
